Cribbage: Color Card Game

Classic two-player cribbage for iPhone and iPad. Race to 121 or play a short game to 61. Practice offline against three computer levels, learn with hints and automatic combination breakdowns, or open a private online table in build 5 and later.

Start a game

Choose the score target and computer level on the home screen. Discard two cards into the dealer’s crib, reveal the starter, then play without taking the count above 31. The app counts fifteens, pairs, runs, flushes and nobs. Saved offline games can be continued from the home screen.

Make it yours

Choose Midnight, Plum, Ocean or Sand on the home screen or in Settings. Card back colors are selected separately. Choose from 49 interface language variants in the globe menu, including regional English, French, Spanish and Portuguese.

Online play

In build 5 and later, create a private table for two to four seats and send the invitation code to your friends. Once at least two people are seated, the host can choose the difficulty of each bot and start; bots fill the remaining seats. At a four-seat table, opposite seats are partners. If your connection drops, the app retries. Open Play online and choose Resume online game after restarting the app. The same device is needed to reclaim the saved seat. Build 4 uses Game Center.
