52 CARDS · TWO PLAYERS

Cribbage: Color Card Game

Classic two-player cribbage for iPhone and iPad. Race to 121 or play a short game to 61. Practice offline against three computer levels, learn with hints and automatic combination breakdowns, or play through Apple Game Center.

Start a game

Choose the score target and computer level on the home screen. Discard two cards into the dealer’s crib, reveal the starter, then play without taking the count above 31. The app counts fifteens, pairs, runs, flushes and nobs. Saved offline games can be continued from the home screen.

Make it yours

Choose Midnight, Plum, Ocean or Sand on the home screen or in Settings. Card back colors are selected separately. English, Russian and Ukrainian are available from the globe menu.

Online play

Both players need Game Center accounts, compatible app versions and matching score targets. Sign in through iOS Settings, then use Play online for automatch or an invitation. Hints and undo are available offline. If a player disconnects, leave and create a new match. Host migration and reconnect after closing the app are not supported.

Support

Report a problem on GitHub. Include the app version, iOS version and steps to reproduce. Do not post passwords or private Apple account details.
