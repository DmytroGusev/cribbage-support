Cribbage — Privacy Policy

Updated 23 October 2026. Developer: DmytroGusev on GitHub.

Local data

Offline game saves, game statistics, language and appearance preferences stay on your device. We do not upload them to a developer server. Device backups may include these files according to your Apple settings. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to backup settings.

Game Center

Online play uses Apple Game Center and GameKit. Your Game Center display name, game-specific player identifier and necessary gameplay state are exchanged with the other participant through Apple services. The host device holds the complete match. The other player receives their own private hand and the public game state; hands and crib are revealed when counted. We do not operate an online account server or store Apple credentials.

Apple processes service data under Apple’s privacy policy. You can manage Game Center through iOS Settings.

Ads and analytics

The app has no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, in-app purchases or real-money wagering. We do not collect gameplay data on our servers. Apple may process diagnostics according to your device and App Store settings.

Support requests

If you submit an issue to the public GitHub support tracker, its content is public and handled under GitHub’s policies. Do not include passwords or private account information. Use the same tracker for privacy questions.
