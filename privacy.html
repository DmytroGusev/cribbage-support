Cribbage — Privacy Policy

Updated 13 November 2026. Developer: DmytroGusev on GitHub.

Local data

Offline game saves, game statistics, language and appearance preferences stay on your device. We do not upload them to a developer server. Device backups may include these files according to your Apple settings. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to backup settings.

Online tables (build 5 and later)

Private online games use Cloudflare to relay the match and resume interrupted play. The room service stores the match state, invitation code, seat assignments and a hash of each temporary seat credential. The app saves the credential locally in Keychain; it is sent only to the room service over encrypted connections. We do not request an account name, email, advertising ID or Game Center identity for these rooms. Other participants see public game events and their own cards, with hands revealed when counted.

The service removes an inactive room from active storage after 24 hours. Cloudflare may retain infrastructure recovery copies under its service terms. IP addresses are used to limit excessive creation and join requests; the room service does not put them in the room record. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Game Center (build 4)

Online play uses Apple Game Center and GameKit. Your Game Center display name, game-specific player identifier and necessary gameplay state are exchanged with the other participant through Apple services. The host device holds the complete match. The other player receives their own private hand and the public game state; hands and crib are revealed when counted. We do not operate an online account server or store Apple credentials.

Apple processes service data under Apple’s privacy policy. You can manage Game Center through iOS Settings.

Ads and analytics

The app has no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, in-app purchases or real-money wagering. Offline gameplay is not sent to the room service. Apple may process diagnostics according to your device and App Store settings.

Support requests

If you submit an issue to the public GitHub support tracker, its content is public and handled under GitHub’s policies. Do not include passwords or private account information. Use the same tracker for privacy questions.
